Advocate High Court — Bahawalpur

Mian Aqil Nawaz is the Owner and Principal Advocate of Mian Law Associates, practising as Advocate High Court from Chamber No. 39, District Courts, Bahawalpur, where the chambers were established in 1997. He represents clients before the District Courts, Special Courts and the Lahore High Court, Bahawalpur Bench.

The chambers were founded in 1997 by Mian Aumer Nawaz, Advocate High Court and Founder Member of Mian Law Associates, whose professional standards continue to guide the firm.

His practice is defined by strategic precision, procedural discipline, and outcome-focused legal representation. Each matter — whether a complex civil suit, a criminal defence, a family court case, or a constitutional writ — is approached with evidentiary rigour and a structured litigation strategy designed to secure decisive and legally sustainable results.

Clients from Bahawalpur, Mailsi, Yazman, Ahmedpur East, Bahawalnagar, Rahim Yar Khan and across South Punjab regularly consult this practice for matters before District Courts, Sessions Courts, Special Courts, and the Lahore High Court, Bahawalpur Bench.
