## How any phrase is built
Searchers combine four parts. Google treats the variants inside each part as the same meaning (synonym system), so a page
needs the main form once in natural text; the other variants are matched automatically.

- **Quality word:** best, good, top, top 10, top 3, expert, experienced, famous, reliable, honest, cheap/affordable ·
  achha, behtareen, mashhoor, tajurbakar, sasta · اچھا، بہترین، مشہور، تجربہ کار، ماہر
- **Person word:** lawyer, advocate, attorney, legal advisor/adviser, law firm, counsel, High Court advocate ·
  wakeel, vakeel, vakil, wakil, advocate sahib · وکیل، ایڈووکیٹ، قانونی مشیر، لاء فرم
- **Case word:** see the areas below
- **Place / form:** "in Bahawalpur", "Bahawalpur", "near me", "for X", "X ka wakeel", "district courts Bahawalpur",
  "Kachahery Chowk", "phone number", "contact" · بہاولپور میں، بہاولپور کا، میرے قریب

Examples: "best lawyer for khula in Bahawalpur", "good advocate for family cases Bahawalpur", "khula ka achha wakeel",
"top criminal lawyer Bahawalpur", "بہاولپور میں فوجداری مقدمات کا بہترین وکیل".

## Rules for adding a phrase
1. Find its family above; it already has a home page. Do not create a new page for a variant.
2. If Search Console shows it brings impressions but the page ranks poorly, add it ONCE in a natural sentence or an FAQ question on the home page, in the next monthly batch, with owner approval.
3. Record the new phrase here with the date.
4. Never add "best" as a claim about MLA (Pakistan Bar Council Rule 135).
