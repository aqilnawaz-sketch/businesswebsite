# MLA 90-Day Search Visibility Roadmap (starts on the go-live date)

Rankings and AI mentions cannot be guaranteed. Progress is measured against the baseline in `MLA-SEO-MONITORING.md`.

## Days 0–7: go live and get indexed (owner actions marked ★)
- ★ Approve the legal text on the 6 rebuilt or new pages; ★ merge the PR.
- ★ Search Console: Domain property `mlabwp.com.pk` (DNS TXT) → submit the sitemap → request indexing of Home, About and the 9 practice pages.
- ★ Bing Webmaster Tools: import from Search Console → submit the sitemap. Then run `scripts/indexnow-submit.sh`.
- Live checks: robots.txt, sitemap, /diary/ 404, redirect stubs, Rich Results Test, PageSpeed (record the baseline).

## Days 7–30: entity consistency
- ★ Google Business Profile: website link → `/`; add each of the 9 services with a link to its practice page; check hours and pin; post weekly.
- ★ Bing Places: claim or import from the Business Profile with an identical name, address and phone.
- ★ LinkedIn, Facebook, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok: identical name, title "Advocate High Court", address and phone; link to https://mlabwp.com.pk and /about/.
- ★ Genuine directories only (e.g. Bahawalpur District Bar, Punjab Bar Council listing if offered). Never paid link schemes.
- Monthly AI prompt test (see `MLA-AI-DISCOVERABILITY.md`) → log the results.

## Days 30–60: content authority
- Two reviewed guides per month, each linked to its practice page. Suggested: succession certificate procedure (NADRA vs court);
  illegal dispossession complaints; maintenance claims in Family Court; haq mehr recovery; service tribunal limitation; PECA complaint process.
- ★ Owner review of each guide; then switch the byline to "Reviewed by Mian Aqil Nawaz".
- Ask satisfied clients for honest Google reviews (no incentives); update `.mla-review-count` when the verified total changes.

## Days 60–90: measure and refine
- Search Console: impressions, clicks, CTR and position per practice page; queries showing cannibalisation → fix per the keyword map.
- Core Web Vitals report (field data); fix any "Poor" URLs.
- Day-90 report: entity (Mian Aqil Nawaz), organisation (MLA), and each of the 9 practice areas, against the baseline.
